Documentation

Everything you need to know.

KibaOS is designed to be simple enough that a manual is optional. This page covers the few things worth knowing, in the order you will meet them.

Installing

Download the image from the downloads page, write it to an 8 GB (or larger) USB stick, boot your computer from it, and follow the guided installer. The full step-by-step walk-through is in the install guide on the home page.

First run

On first boot, KibaOS walks you through a short guided setup: choose your language and your accessibility options, and you are done. Everything else works out of the box.

Everyday use

The KibaDE desktop gives you the Chromium browser, the Apps and Games store, the Files app, and everyday tools like a calculator and notes. Open the store to find what you need — no terminal required, though it is there if you want it.

Updates

KibaOS updates quietly in the background. There is no update center to babysit — the system stays current on its own and lets you know when a restart is actually needed.

Under the hood

Curious what KibaOS is built from? The technical overview describes the KibaDE desktop environment (ChromiumOS Ash, via the Lumen fork of Chromium) and the Arch Linux base. Common questions are answered in the FAQ.
