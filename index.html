Quick answers.

What is KibaOS?

It's the software that runs your computer — the screen you see and the buttons you press. It comes with a browser, a file manager and an app store.

Can I try it without installing?

Yes. It runs from a USB stick, so you can explore everything before anything on your computer changes.

How do updates work?

Small updates install quietly in the background. No big yearly upgrade.

Does it spy on me?

No. Nothing is collected unless you say yes, and you can simply say no.

Is it really free?

Yes, free forever. Downloads are on SourceForge.

Ready to try it?

Download KibaOS, put it on a USB stick, and see for yourself.
