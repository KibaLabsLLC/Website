04 — Questions

Before you ask.

What is KibaOS?

It's the software that runs your computer once it starts up — the screen you see, the buttons you press. KibaOS comes with a browser, a place for your files, and an app store, all set up and ready to go the moment you turn it on.

Can I try it without installing?

Yes. KibaOS runs straight from a USB stick, so you can explore the whole desktop — and check that everything works on your computer — before anything is saved or changed.

How do updates work?

Small updates arrive regularly and install in the background. There's no big once-a-year upgrade to sit through, and you never have to start over from scratch.

Does it spy on me?

No. Nothing is ever collected without asking you first — and you can simply say no. If you do opt in, it's only anonymous info that helps the team fix problems and support more computers.

Is it really free?

Completely — free today, free forever. It's built in the open by a small team, and anyone can look at exactly how it's made. Downloads are hosted on SourceForge.

Ready for a calmer desktop?

Download KibaOS, put it on a USB stick, and see for yourself how good simple can feel. The lake is lovely this time of night.
