Our mission

A calm, simple computer for everyone.

Kiba Labs, LLC — founded in Aurora, Missouri, USA — exists to put a calm, simple desktop in front of everyone who sits down at a computer. We believe software should feel like a good tool: it starts fast, stays out of the way, and never makes you feel lost.

That belief is why KibaOS is free and open source under the GPL v3, why it never collects or sells your data, and why every feature must pass one test first: does it make the system simpler to use?

What we believe

Four ideas, one shape of mind.

The values behind everything Kiba Labs makes.

Calm by default

No nagging, no noise. Software that stays out of your way.

Privacy first

We do not collect or sell your data. Not now, not ever.

Free and open

GPL v3, free forever. Read it, change it, share it.

Friendly to everyone

Language and accessibility options come first, not last.

How we work

Small team, open doors.

We build in the open

Everything we make is developed publicly on GitHub. Source, issues, and decisions are visible to anyone — including the parts that prove KibaOS does not phone home.

We keep it simple

KibaOS has one goal above all: to be as simple to use as possible. When a feature and simplicity disagree, simplicity wins.

We answer our email

Questions, feedback, and ideas go straight to corporate@kibaos.com — read by the same people who build the product.

What we make

KibaOS.

Our one product: a free, user-friendly desktop that pairs the KibaDE environment — based on ChromiumOS Ash, built from the Lumen fork of Chromium — with an Arch Linux base.

  • DesktopKibaDE (ChromiumOS Ash via Lumen)
  • BaseArch Linux
