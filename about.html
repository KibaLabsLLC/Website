About the company

Kiba Labs, LLC.

A small company with a simple goal: computing that's calm, kind and open to everyone.

Kiba Labs, LLC is the company behind KibaOS, a free and open-source desktop that's ready to go the moment you turn your computer on.

We started Kiba Labs because computers have become stressful — missing drivers, nagging updates and menus that hide everything. KibaOS is our answer: a quiet, friendly desktop that comes with the web, your files and an app store already set up.

KibaOS is free forever, licensed under the GNU GPL, and shaped every day by the people who use it. If something feels wrong, you can tell us — and it can change.

What we believe

Calm software, open by design.

Calm by default

No pop-ups, no nagging, no dashboards you never asked for. KibaOS stays out of your way.

Free and open source

KibaOS is licensed under the GNU GPL. Read it, change it, share it — that's the point.

Privacy first

Nothing is collected unless you say yes, and you can simply say no. Your computer is yours.

Friendly to everyone

Plain language, big buttons, patient tooltips. Computers should welcome beginners, not test them.
