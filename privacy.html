3. KibaOS Telemetry

Certain KibaOS components may provide optional telemetry intended to help Kiba Labs understand hardware compatibility, reliability, and general product usage.

Telemetry is designed to be opt-in. KibaOS should not transmit telemetry when the required user consent has not been provided.

Depending on the version and configuration of KibaOS, telemetry may include aggregated technical information such as hardware manufacturer, CPU category, GPU category, motherboard or system manufacturer, and software or system version information.

Kiba Labs does not intend for this telemetry to contain directly identifying hardware information such as MAC addresses, serial numbers, or network identifiers.

Telemetry may be used to understand hardware compatibility, identify reliability issues, improve KibaOS, and guide future development.

The exact information collected may vary between releases. Kiba Labs will document material changes to telemetry practices when appropriate.
