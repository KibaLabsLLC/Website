Questions

Quick answers.

What is KibaOS?

It's the software that runs your computer — the screen you see and the buttons you press. It comes with a browser, a file manager and an app store. Curious about the details? See the technical overview.

Can I try it without installing?

Yes. It runs from a USB stick, so you can explore everything before anything on your computer changes.

How do updates work?

Small updates install quietly in the background. No big yearly upgrade.

Does it spy on me?

No, KibaOS does not collect or sell your data.

Is it really free?

Yes, free forever. Downloads are on SourceForge.
