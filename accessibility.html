Accessibility

Accessibility.

Accessibility is part of first-run setup, not an afterthought — and that applies to this website too.

In KibaOS

The first time KibaOS boots, guided setup asks you to choose your accessibility options alongside your language — before you reach the desktop. What you pick is what the system uses from the first screen onward.

On this website

The site is built to be usable by everyone:

Semantic HTML with landmarks and a skip-to-content link; visible keyboard focus states; full keyboard navigation; prefers-reduced-motion is respected, so scroll and entrance animations are disabled if your system requests it; text alternatives are provided for meaningful images; color contrast is chosen for readability; and content reflows cleanly on small screens and at zoom.

Tell us what we missed

If something on this website or in KibaOS is hard to see, hear, or use, that is a bug. Email corporate@kibaos.com and describe what got in your way — accessibility reports are treated as priority fixes.
